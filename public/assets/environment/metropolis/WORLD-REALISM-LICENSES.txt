AEGIS world-realism additions - original work, 2026-10-15

urban-details.blend, build_street_details.py and street-details.glb:
Original AEGIS project authoring created for this workspace. No external model,
image or proprietary game content was copied into these new modules.
Includes newspaper dispenser, dumpster, fence, planter, awning, pedestrian signal,
street sign, service vent, opaque tree crowns and street-lamp lens modules.
Runtime street-details-optimized.glb is a Meshopt-compressed derivative.

StreetMaterials.ts uses the project's existing asphalt PBR maps. No new texture
license is introduced. StreetSigns.ts draws original AEGIS street names and
simple sign graphics into one shared canvas atlas; no downloaded imagery/font.
WetStreetReflections.ts reuses the existing attributed source-facades atlas.
The original building authors and source licenses remain applicable to reflections.
See public/assets/environment/metropolis/LICENSES.txt and asset-usage.json,
art/environment/phase7/provided and art/external for retained source attribution.
Existing source buildings and their textures remain under their original licenses.

concrete-color.webp, concrete-normal.webp, concrete-rough.webp and concrete-height.png:
Original deterministic tileable aggregate synthesis from build_concrete.py,
seed 72015. No third-party image inputs; editable height source retained.
