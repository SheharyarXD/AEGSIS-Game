AEGIS Phase 10 original authored additions

The corridor office partitions, damaged stone vaults, service pipes, structural frames,
ceiling trays and entrance canopies in phase10-cinematic-kit.glb and its optimized
derivative were authored specifically for this project using the Blender Python API.
Editable source: art/environment/phase10/cinematic-corridors.blend
Reproduction source: art/environment/phase10/build_cinematic_kit.py

These additions contain no downloaded game assets, copied proprietary architecture,
third-party texture downloads or franchise-specific character material. The project
owner may use and modify these original additions with AEGIS.

Runtime materials reuse the existing project's licensed PBR atlases. Their existing
attribution and terms remain authoritative: ../ATTRIBUTION.txt, LICENSES.txt,
and the source texture notices under art/environment/ and public/assets/environment/.
Original supplied architecture and character asset licenses are unchanged.
