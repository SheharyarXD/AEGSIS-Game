What changed

  • Dedicated road, concrete, patched asphalt and worn-paint materials; tileable 512 px concrete color/normal/roughness, stable slab joints, curb gaps, approach bars, arrows and patchy wet roughness.
  • A deliberately furnished hero street at X=232, Z=-496…-336, plus district frontage/corner/service rules citywide. Newspaper dispensers, planters, service dumpsters, fences, awnings, pedestrian signals, street-name atlas, finer opaque trees and lamp lenses are instanced from the authored kit.
  • Fixed-budget lighting follows actual street fixtures; day sun/ambient balance has more contrast. Wet streets use limited source-atlas facade proxies and light streaks. There is no planar reflection or SSR scene pass.
  • Four pooled draws for ragged scorch, three turbulent flame tongues per fire, wind-advected smoke/dust billboards and short-lived sparks/flash. Repeated damage grows the localized fire/plume. Source-owner ray queries reattach effects to surviving surfaces; missing surfaces clear them.
  • Structural support loss emits capped dust bursts. Existing source panel debris, sparse triangle updates, component collision, collapse restrictions, settling and streaming persistence are preserved. Canopy collision sits at canopy height; rotated props receive rotated collision.

Verification and practical limits

31 player/HUD/combat/camera/audio/input/physics/config source files match their starting SHA-256. 40 browser tests passed in the latest report; see the raw result for final status. The local package is 268.7 MiB including DDS and WebP alternatives and all runtime families; actual browsers request the supported format and streamed subsets. Large physics/Three.js chunks remain. No host upload was performed.

Remaining limits: vehicles and some vegetation/props retain stylized geometry; this is not an AAA visual-completion claim. Wet reflection proxies do not track dynamic objects or exact view-dependent reflections. Smoke uses noise billboards without scene-depth soft intersections or volumetric lighting. Small facade chips use a source-component boundary, not arbitrary runtime mesh fracture. Damage state persists through streaming; session persistence is exposed by the existing serialization API rather than a new save-game feature. Dense traffic/pedestrian AI, every weather/quality/scene combination, mobile browsers and portal acceptance are outside these measurements.
