Known limits

  • Artistic grouped collapse with four kinematic source masses, not engineering-grade rigid-body structural simulation.
  • Near source-derived facade patches and three persistent convex ruin hulls approximate major remains. Smaller debris is capped, sleeps, and consolidates.
  • Intact city remains the saved 971-building, five-district plan with 25 authored architectural families. Broad avenues, repeating source families and simple interiors still need further art direction.
  • Smoke uses pooled animated density shaders and flames use analytic plume fields; no volumetric fluid simulation or ray-traced reflections.
  • Final timing runs share the GPU with an Android emulator using about 1.3 GB VRAM, as requested. Frame stalls above 100 ms remain in some shared-GPU heavy scenes; no zero-stutter or locked-60 claim is made.
  • MEDIUM is primary; HIGH and ULTRA use more internal pixels and are not guaranteed to reach 60 FPS on a GTX 750 Ti.
  • Preflight rejects obstructed roofs/adjacent exits; no collision bypass. Y has its own finite 8-round ability load paid with 48 reactor energy; ordinary missile magazine remains independent.
  • Third-party credits and license terms remain applicable. No new third-party assets downloaded in Phase 9.
