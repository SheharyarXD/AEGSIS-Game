Remaining limits and retained failures

  • Not an AAA-finished city: repeated families/corridors, broad avenues and simplified urban life remain.
  • Nearby cinematics take roughly 7–9 s; a long rooftop approach can take around 14 s.
  • Measurements share the GPU with unrelated desktop applications. The emulator was left untouched but is not listed in the final allocation samples. Baseline contention differs; no controlled percentage-speed claim.
  • Cold streaming and activation outliers are measured, not guaranteed below a hard budget.
  • Architectural refraction is deliberately approximated by reflective glazing. Other reflections, dust, flames and collapse are bounded approximations.
  • Raw Playwright recordings are silent; audible mix quality is not established.
  • Portal network/download certification, every device/GPU, and inherited source/pilot redistribution licenses remain outside these local results.
