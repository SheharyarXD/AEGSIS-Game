Changes: saved city parcels; uniformly scaled source architecture; UV-preserving
floor/facade separation; independent damage IDs; hidden structural additions;
near/medium LODs; Blender-rendered source-view distant atlas; shared native BC3
textures with WebP fallback; authored sidewalks, drains and original furniture;
bounded source-derived debris and burned/damaged state proxies.

The intact near appearance uses the complete source model. Distant atlas cards
are a performance LOD, not replacement source architecture. Full-detail Blender
sources and standalone GLBs with embedded PBR maps are retained for inspection.

Other supplied assets remain inspected but unused; the Sketchfab Standard modern
city block and Quixel-like apartment pack are not included in the runtime city.
